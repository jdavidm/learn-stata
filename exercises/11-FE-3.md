Using `tenuredata.dta` (restricted to `rice == 1`):

1. Instead of manually demeaning the data, use the `i.` operator to include a fixed effect dummy for every individual household (`panelid`).
2. Run a regression of raw `yield` on `q_f_ha` and your dummy variables (`i.panelid`). Cluster the standard errors by `panelid`.
3. Does the coefficient on `q_f_ha` exactly match your result from Exercise 2 (Demeaning)?
